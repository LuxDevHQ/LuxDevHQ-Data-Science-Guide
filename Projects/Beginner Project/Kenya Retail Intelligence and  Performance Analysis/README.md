### **Kenya Retail Intelligence and Performance Analysis at LT Lab**

### **Project Scenario**

Imagine you are working as a **Data Analyst at LT Lab**, a data and technology consulting company.

LT Lab has been contracted by **Adventure Distributor Kenya**, a growing retail and distribution company operating across several counties in Kenya, to analyze its sales, customer, inventory, branch, supplier, and operational data.

Adventure Distributor Kenya has experienced significant growth over the last few years. The company now operates multiple branches, serves thousands of customers, manages a large catalogue of products, works with several suppliers, and processes transactions through channels such as physical stores, online orders, and WhatsApp orders.

Customers also pay through different payment methods, including **M-Pesa, cash, card, and bank transfer**.

As the company has grown, management has found it increasingly difficult to answer important business questions using raw transactional data alone.

The leadership team wants to better understand:

- How much revenue the business is generating
- Whether sales are growing or declining
- Which branches are performing best
- Which counties generate the most revenue
- Which products and product categories are most profitable
- Which customers contribute the most value to the business
- How important M-Pesa is to the company's revenue
- Which products are fast-moving and which are slow-moving
- Whether some branches are carrying too much or too little inventory
- Which products are approaching their reorder levels
- How operating expenses affect branch profitability
- Which suppliers support the most valuable products
- Whether customer purchasing behaviour differs across locations and customer segments

Adventure Distributor Kenya has therefore asked **LT Lab** to transform the company's raw operational data into meaningful business insights.

You have been assigned as the **Data Analyst responsible for this engagement**.

Your task is to use **Microsoft Excel** to clean, transform, analyze, and visualize the provided datasets and ultimately build an **Executive Retail Performance Dashboard** that management can use to make informed business decisions.

You will work with several related datasets covering:

- Sales transactions
- Customers
- Products
- Branches
- Inventory
- Suppliers
- Sales employees
- Operating expenses
- Payment methods
- Customer locations

The project contains tens of thousands of records, meaning that simply scrolling through the data will not be enough.

You will need to apply the Excel skills you have learned throughout the course, including:

- Excel Tables
- Data cleaning
- Sorting and filtering
- Conditional formatting
- `IF` and logical functions
- `SUMIFS`
- `COUNTIFS`
- `AVERAGEIFS`
- `XLOOKUP`
- Date and text functions
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Slicers
- Power Query
- Data relationships
- Business KPI calculations
- Dashboard design

By the end of this project, you should be able to move from **raw business data** to a professional analysis that helps Adventure Distributor Kenya understand what is happening in the business, why it is happening, and where management should focus its attention.
